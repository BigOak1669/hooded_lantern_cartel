# Shield of the Lion

Sab recovered this shield from the old exercise room in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton]] in [[2026_1108 Session 22|Session 22]]. Its metal face depicts a roaring lion with a flowing mane. It adjusted to fit her and is a **+1 shield** when equipped. Sab equipped it and put away her previous shield. In [[2026_1115 Session 23|Session 23]], [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah]] identified it through the curtained portal. Once per day, Sab can speak a command word to animate the lion and make it roar, intimidating nearby hostile creatures. Any exact range or saving throw was unspecified. Sab left her previous shield in the first-floor entrance guardroom.
