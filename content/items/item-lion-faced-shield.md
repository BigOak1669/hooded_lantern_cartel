# Lion-Faced Shield

Sab recovered this shield from the old exercise room in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton]] in [[2026_1108 Session 22|Session 22]]. Its metal face depicts a roaring lion with a flowing mane. It adjusted to fit her and is a **+1 shield** when equipped. It has additional magical properties; the party must meet with [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah]] to identify them. Sab equipped it and put away her previous shield.
