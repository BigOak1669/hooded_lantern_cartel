# Item: Staff of the Cobra

## Summary

A fine dark-wood +1 staff topped by a carved cobra head, recovered from a lectern between two lizardfolk statues in the [[location-broken-sigil-mound|broken-sigil mound]].

## Observed Properties

- Removing it caused several small snakes to emerge.
- When [[pc-oogie|Oogie]] pointed it at the snakes, they shifted direction but continued following the party.
- [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah]] established that [[pc-oogie|Oogie]] can throw it down once per day to transform it into a large serpent servant.
- Snakes regard Oogie as friendly.
- When Oogie accidentally touched the marked mound's door, the painted serpent treated him as friendly and slipped away rather than attacking.

- In Session 19 Wade confirmed that the cobra returns to staff form if killed; someone else could then pick it up. The staff was not permanently consumed during the earlier marked-mound use.
- Oogie summoned it at Quasqueton, and its bite finished the upper-nest harpy after Grond's critical arrow. Commanding it did not cost Oogie's own action in that encounter.

## Current Status

Held by Oogie. It was last explicitly in cobra form in Quasqueton's upper tower; discussion of returning it to staff form did not clearly resolve that action. Its established use is once per day. At the end of Session 19 Wade offered a once-per-game allowance. He subsequently confirmed it was session-specific, with the intent to correct the rules going forward; the established daily limit remains the ongoing rule.
