# Talisman of the Beastmaster

A leather cord strung with old claws, bone beads, and one large pearly claw, found in Tornar's rooms and now held by Orlin. Mazzah identified it as a Talisman of the Beastmaster. While worn, it grants +1 to claw, fang, and other natural attacks.

In [[2026_1011 Session 20|Session 20]], Orlin described and activated its once-daily ability: after a successful claw wound it can attract a nearby ordinary animal, curious but uncontrollable. The plant-rat guide returned after the centipede kill, but whether the ability caused that return was not established.
