# Tongue of the Beast

A wooden tongue found in Tornar's rooms and now held by Orlin. Mazzah identified it as a Tongue of the Beast. Its user places it in the mouth and spends a turn focusing on one kind of animal, after which the user can communicate with that animal type.

Orlin used it on the Quasqueton approach to speak with a fox. The fox said winged hunters made it afraid to fish at the lake.

In [[2026_1011 Session 20|Session 20]], Orlin focused it on the altered rat guide and then spoke with the rats in Greenheart's nursery. He learned that strong light hurt the guide's eyes and that Greenheart would judge the strangers' friendship. This use did not allow him to speak directly with Greenheart, which was a plant.
