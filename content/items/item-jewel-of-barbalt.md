# Jewel of Barbalt

Sab found this coconut-sized cut ruby in a wooden display case inside a trapped desk drawer in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] hidden study. In [[2026_1115 Session 23|Session 23]], [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah]] identified it through the curtained portal.

When used, the jewel makes a natural d20 roll of 18–20 a critical success and a natural 1–3 a critical failure. The table confirmed that these ranges use the face of the die before modifiers. The exact activation and scope of the effect were left open.

[[pc-sab|Sab]] is keeping the cased jewel in the [[item-bag-of-holding|bag of holding]] for safekeeping. She has not started using it.
