# Beetle Potion of Animal Shifting

[[pc-grond|Grond]] found this opaque, beetle-shaped bottle in the middle drawer of a clothes cabinet in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] hidden study.

In [[2026_1115 Session 23|Session 23]], [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah]] identified it as a Potion of Animal Shifting. He said the drinker would temporarily become a beetle of ordinary size. The exact duration was unspecified. Grond kept the potion and has not used it.
