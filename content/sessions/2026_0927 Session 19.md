## Executive Summary

The party completed its overnight rest in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] entrance guardroom, then explored a vaulted fountain hall. Fresh seed husks, oversized rodent tracks, and faint bitter incense indicated activity inside. To make outside provisioning safer, the group chose to clear the upper harpy nest. Grond critically wounded its lone visible occupant with an arrow; Oogie's summoned cobra finished it. The party found no further harpies nearby and ended play in the cleared upper tower. They split 42 gold pieces, while Sab took an ornate silver-finished hand mirror and Oogie took a nonmagical sword-and-star belt buckle.

## What Happened

The group made a campfire and took watches in the entrance guardroom. Sab's gathered berries covered her ration; the remaining campers used their provisions. Sab found an old rat hole with fresh seed husks beside it and plugged it with an unburned torch, intending to retrieve the torch afterward. Gradrick put the [[item-crystal-owl|Owl of Magnus]] outside the door to watch the hallway through its eyes and heard distant female laughter on the wind from above. He retrieved the owl before moving on. Grond found a faded sword-and-star mark associated with Rogahn and Zelligar. During the final watch Oogie felt cold air through the door and smelled something faint, bitter, and incense-like. Morning arrived without an attack, and [[npc-katherine-guy|Katherine Guy]] woke with the party.

Oogie smelled the incense again near the swollen double doors deeper inside; Grond independently confirmed it. It seemed to come from farther inside the stronghold. Oogie foresaw possible quiet and noisy openings of the doors, both leading into darkness. Grond eased one door open with little noise. Katherine took Sab's lit torch, and Gradrick lit his lantern.

Beyond lay a large hall under a high vaulted ceiling. Sab examined the shallow fountain basin: its bottom bore the builders' sword-and-star emblem, and a small drain appeared plugged. The party found an ascending stair beneath an ornate arch and a hallway leading farther in. Oogie noticed rodent tracks in the dust leading down that hallway, large enough to suggest a medium dog or larger. Similar tracks ran up and down the stairs. Their makers were not seen.

Sab argued that clearing the harpies would let the group safely gather food, wood, and torch material outside. The others agreed to go upstairs. Daylight entered through arrow slits in a broad circular upper hall, where scattered bones, feathers, and debris grew thicker near the nest. Katherine voiced concern about the harpies and was instructed to stay quietly near the stairs while the party approached.

Gradrick sent forward a flying, bat-winged frog familiar and stayed back himself. Oogie cast Rage on Grond, with a critical casting extending its duration to five rounds; Wade allowed it to continue during the stealthy approach despite the spell's normal attack requirement. Sab cast Protection from Evil on Gradrick and then Oogie. After discussing its focus restriction, Wade allowed both protections for this encounter.

Grond approached quietly and spotted one harpy facing away, apparently eating something in a raised nest of bones, fur, feathers, and glittering scraps. Oogie transformed the [[item-cobra-headed-staff|Staff of the Cobra]] into a giant cobra and sent it forward. Grond shot the harpy in the back of the head for 13 damage with a critical hit. It thrashed in the nest, scattering coins and debris. Oogie's cobra bit it for the final point of damage, killing it before it made a described attack or song attempt.

Sab searched for evidence of more harpies and found no further activity nearby or eggs in the nest. The party felt confident it had secured the immediate exterior from harpy attacks, with three harpies killed across Sessions 18 and 19. Sab found an ornate silver-finished hand mirror; Gradrick recovered 42 gold pieces in total, including the initial nine he had found; Oogie took a heavy, nonmagical belt buckle bearing the sword-and-star emblem. The nest also contained glass scraps and bones of fish, deer, foxes, oversized rodents, and humanoids. The coins were divided as 10 each to Sab, Oogie, and Gradrick, and 12 to Grond. Play ended in the upper tower after the nest was cleared.
