## Executive Summary

The party finished searching the serpent-and-skull marked mound. Sab gave Mara's remains dignity and returned her wedding cord to Mort, whom Grond helped into a bed to sleep off the fortified ale. The party then opened the eastern crypt, destroyed four unusually aggressive zombies, and survived an ambush by one large and two smaller clockwork snakes hidden among the grave goods. They salvaged gems, a mechanical snake heart, a precision spring, and one dose of venom, and recovered thirty-five gold pieces. A corpse in an ornate coat carried the name of Eldran Vey, steward of Quasqueton, along with papers the party took for later study. The north altar remained unopened. The party left the mound safely with the Owl of Magnus, its loot, and its recovered lore; the next session will open as they arrive in Helix. Mort's final disposition was not explicitly settled.

## What Happened

The session resumed in Tornar's western workroom. Mort remained drunk, exhausted, and distraught over Mara. When Sab asked Mara's name, Mort answered and repeated that he had only wanted his wife back. Sab improvised a funeral observance, gathered the remaining grave cloth out of sight, and found a silver wedding cord among Mara's remains. Grond helped Mort to the better bed in the room, and Sab placed the cord in Mort's hand before he fell asleep.

The party reviewed the blue cloud-filled potion but left it unidentified. With the western rooms already searched, they returned to the central hall. Orlin used Nara's keys on the eastern door. After noticing movement through the keyhole and hearing undead sounds, he unlocked the door and let Grond enter first.

The eastern room held four ravenous zombies rather than the slow household dead the party had just faced. Oogie's trance showed Grond smashing the first zombie, giving Grond an advantage when he entered. Grond struck it, and Sab destroyed it with her new Smite spell. Another zombie rushed Orlin and tore into him; Orlin transformed into a bear and ripped it apart. Grond crushed a third zombie wearing an ornate coat with his sledgehammer. Sab burned the fourth with another Smite. No undead remained active.

The cleared chamber looked less like a crypt than a thieves' storeroom. Gold lay around a sarcophagus and two ornate amphorae, and a faint ticking came from bright metal coils wrapped around the vessels. When the party examined the display, the coils unfolded into a large clockwork cobra and two smaller clockwork snakes. Their bites carried venom.

The constructs wounded Grond, Sab, and Gradrick. Orlin put himself between Gradrick and the large cobra and fought it in bear form. Sab cut one smaller snake in half with her longsword, Grond smashed the other, and Gradrick finished the main cobra with Eye Bite after Orlin tore away part of its metal plating. Oogie used an omen to restore luck to Gradrick and Orlin during the fight. Sab healed Gradrick afterward and later restored Orlin to full health.

The party salvaged the constructs. Orlin removed two ruby eyes and a fragile spring-loaded mechanical heart from the main cobra. Oogie recovered two smaller gemstone eyes, a precision spring, and an intact venom reservoir from the cleanly cut snake. Grond found one remaining gemstone eye from the snake he had crushed. Gradrick and Sab gathered thirty-five gold pieces from the chamber.

The corpse's ornate coat bore a brass plate identifying him as Eldran Vey, steward of Quasqueton and keeper of its road, spring, and outer wall. That identification strengthens the connection to the existing Hollow Gate of Quasqueton writ. Gradrick took the coat and the scrolls or notes found on the body. The papers were not read aloud during the session, so their detailed contents remain unknown to the campaign record.

The party chose not to open the warm north altar or descend beneath it. They left safely for Helix, recovered the Owl of Magnus on the way out, and discussed three possible directions for a future expedition: return to the barrows through the altar descent, investigate Quasqueton under the Hollow Gate writ, or pursue the separate distant-castle lead. No destination was firmly chosen. The table handled level-up bookkeeping after deciding to return, but the exact experience award for this session was not finalized aloud. The next session will open with the party arriving safely in Helix.
