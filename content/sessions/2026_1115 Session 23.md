# Session Note

## Executive Summary

The party explored a hidden study off [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] pool chamber, recovering papers, journals, 90 gold, two healing potions, and the Jewel of Barbalt. After resupplying at [[npc-lumpy|Lumpy's]], the group found a smithy, a water shaft, a repairs cache, and a curtained portal into [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah's]] sitting room. Mazzah identified several finds through the opening. The party finished its upper-floor sweep and set up camp in the first-floor entrance guardroom. The session awarded seven XP.

## What Happened

Behind the rotating wall lay a dry bedroom and study. Katherine raised her torch to illuminate a large portrait of a scarred warrior, a thin wizard in purple, and a gray-haired elf. The plaque dedicates the portrait to Marevak for guiding the two signatories, Rogahn and Zelligar. Orlin understood Marevak to be the elf in the middle. Orlin gathered loose papers from the desk and mentioned “IX” and Zelligar. Gradrick took journals from a shelf and said an entry suggested that the wizard's experiments with roots had something to do with the green life. The group saved the papers and journals to read at camp.

Oogie found a leather chest under the bed containing 90 gold and two matching red potions. Sab triggered a hissing desk-drawer gas trap that induced an urge to flee. Sab, Orlin, and Katherine resisted it. Sab checked the wooden display case in the drawer and found a coconut-sized magical ruby inside. She stored the case in the bag of holding. Grond searched a separate chest of drawers and pocketed an opaque beetle-shaped bottle.

Pale vines or roots glowed along cracks in the upper corridor, enough for the group to retrace a familiar stretch without its own lights. The party stayed together and reopened Lumpy's door. Sab paid 10 gold for 18 torches, and the group also resupplied with lamp oil and rations. They continued exploring the upper floor before returning to camp.

In a guest room, what appeared to be a sleeping person under the covers gave Oogie no psychic response. Grond lifted the sheets with his hammer and the illusion stopped. Orlin picked up a silver-colored cloak clasp. Beyond the guest room, the group reached a charcoal storeroom with usable torch-making supplies, a smithy with forges and an anvil, and a damp water-shaft room. The shaft drops roughly 30 feet to the first floor and continues downward beyond sight. The party observed rodent tracks through these service rooms.

After Oogie restored Sab's luck, she felt drawn west and noticed wear leading into a wall. Gradrick found how the panel moved, and Grond opened it onto a cache of beams, spare doors, dressed stone, and rope. Farther along, Katherine noticed a well-kept door. Orlin opened it onto a purple velvet curtain, and Oogie drew it back to reveal Mazzah taking tea with a spectral, rainbow-maned pony. The pony vanished when the party called out.

Gradrick sent Splat through and back, and the party passed items to Mazzah for identification. Mazzah said the connection was new to him. He wondered aloud about his tapestries and refreshed the party's torches. With the curtain drawn, the party could still hear him faintly, but he could not find the opening during the test. Sab drew the curtain back from Quasqueton's side. The party continued past ruined workers' quarters, took the route back to the entrance, and set up camp in the same guardroom used earlier in the expedition.

- **Study papers and journals** carried by Orlin and Gradrick, respectively. Orlin also kept the cloak clasp, which Mazzah judged ordinary.
- **Seven XP** awarded for the jewel and potions. The table confirmed that XP carries over into the next level. One player reported reaching level four, but the speaker is unclear.
