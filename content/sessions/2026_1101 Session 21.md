# Session Note

<div class="quick-recap-label">Quick Read</div>

## Executive Summary

The party left [[npc-greenheart|Greenheart's]] nursery and explored more of [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] upper corridors. They subdued a many-eyed, arachnid-orc hunter and used Oogie's rapport spores to perceive a great spiderlike female controlling similar creatures and the hunter's duty to catch rats for food. After the bound hunter spat corrosive-looking venom at Orlin, Oogie killed it with Fate. Behind the concealed entrance it had been investigating, the group found four adult greenkin mothers and roughly thirty young in a decayed storeroom. Orlin reassured them, gave a ration, and recovered a sepia gemstone estimated at 30 gold. Oogie's fungal contact showed a greenkin drinking from a gray stone pool, stiffening, and dying. Play ended as the group regrouped at an old exercise room. No captives or heart seed were recovered; no XP was awarded.

## What Happened

The opening recap revisited the conditional agreement with Greenheart: try to recover its children or secure peace, then return a promised heart seed north. No seed had been handed over. Sab's plant-speaking draught had worn off. Orlin tried taking glowing moss from the nursery wall, but it lost its light when detached. Katherine Guy lit a torch, and Sab cast Light on her shield, which glowed especially brightly after a critical success.

The group passed the pristine doorway associated with Lumpy's shop without reopening it. They followed branching upper corridors and chose east at a junction. Orlin sensed something ahead at the edge of the light and signaled caution. The approaching light revealed a large humanoid with many eyes, arachnid facial features and shell-like skin, a hooked spear, and a web-like net. It had been concentrating on a section of wall and hissed when it noticed the party.

Oogie cast Fate for ten damage, leaving the creature convulsing on one knee. Gradrick cast Hypnotize and stupefied it. Sab noticed that the wall concealed some sort of entrance. Grond guarded the rear, suspecting something behind the party; no rear attacker was revealed. Orlin tried focusing the Tongue of the Beast on the hunter, but it was too humanoid for the item to establish animal speech.

Gradrick failed to maintain the hypnosis, even after spending luck. Sab threatened the recovering creature with her crossbow; it dropped its spear and net and raised its hands. Orlin displayed his claws and addressed it in Common without obtaining an intelligible response. Katherine renewed her torch and stayed near the group despite her fear.

Oogie released rapport spores, linking himself, Sab, Orlin, and the hunter for psychic communication. They perceived a great female humanoid-spider figure in a cavernous space, controlling orcish creatures with differing arachnid traits. The hunter conveyed regret at failing its duty. Sab probed further and understood that the duty involved food and catching rats. Grond and Gradrick were not direct participants in the link; the vision was described at the table for them on the understanding that the others would relay it.

The party debated killing the hunter, seeking its controller, and whether another food source or killing the controller might end the predation. Gradrick, aided by Splat, tied the hunter's hands and feet. It struggled without escaping. Orlin warned it that the party would kill the spiderlike controller to stop the rats being eaten, hoping this might free the others. The hunter spat viscous purple liquid at him. Orlin dodged, and the liquid fizzed against the stone behind him. Oogie's second Fate casting dealt eight damage and killed it. No party injury was described.

After the break, Grond examined the concealed entrance and enlarged a small hole covered with stones, leaves, and other material chosen to blend into the wall. He entered with Katherine's light. Gradrick lit his lantern and supplied a wider view. The hidden room was an abandoned storeroom: old barrels and their contents had largely decomposed into compost, though boards still bore food labels. Four large, leaf-eared greenkin mothers sheltered with roughly thirty young. They were afraid, apparently after the hunter's attention at their doorway, and showed no aggression.

Orlin focused the wooden tongue on the greenkin. He told them that the party knew Greenheart, had killed the many-eyed hunter outside, and meant them no harm. They relaxed. Sab examined the enlarged entrance to work out how to conceal it again and discovered that it was already healing shut. Loose material seemed to be drawn back into the mass; she warned the others to leave if they had finished.

Grond found a second small opening leading to another corridor but returned through the first entrance. Orlin noticed a reddish-brown glint among the decayed barrels. A mother encouraged him to inspect and share it; he petted her, gave a ration for the young, and uncovered a sizable sepia-colored gemstone. Its estimated value was 30 gold, and it could go in his coin pouch without a separate gear slot. Oogie successfully used Trance to restore Gradrick's luck token, choosing one recipient despite a critical success.

Oogie examined more barrels, finding labels for pickles, fish, salt, lard, seasoning, and mushrooms. Through contact with the mushrooms, without needing to eat them, he perceived a gray stone pool filled with grayish liquid. A greenkin drank, cramped, stiffened, and died. Oogie told the others about what he called a poison pool. Its location and the liquid's actual nature were not established.

Sab and Gradrick waited outside near Katherine. Grond crawled out as the first opening continued to close. Orlin and Oogie used the second small passage and emerged in the adjacent corridor, seeing their companions coming around the corner. No greenkin were moved, and these mothers and young were not established to be the missing captives.

The group chose to inspect the eastern door rather than continue south. Grond forced the swollen wooden door with rusty iron fittings, making it squeak. Gradrick brought his lantern forward, revealing weapon holders, a bench with a bar, weighted bars, dumbbell-like weights, and old brittle mats: an apparent exercise room or dojo. Orlin moved up while watching that Oogie was not left alone. Play stopped at this room, with Oogie approaching from the south and Katherine staying behind Grond.

The closing discussion focused on light supplies and whether to take inventory before getting too far from the shop. Wade estimated that roughly half a day had passed since the party left its campsite. No return to the shop, rest, descent, or further room search occurred.
