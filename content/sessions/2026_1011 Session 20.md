# Session Note

<div class="quick-recap-label">Quick Read</div>

## Executive Summary

The party explored [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] upper passages, killed two venomous centipedes, and followed a plant-altered rat to [[npc-greenheart|Greenheart's]] nursery. A pristine doorway also opened onto [[npc-lumpy|Lumpy's]] familiar equipment storeroom, where Oogie bought rope. Sab drank Mazzah's plant-speaking draught and negotiated with Greenheart, which reported that web-using, many-eyed beings below were stealing its children. A shared sap vision showed a purple-robed wizard stealing a heart seed from an elven forest city. The party agreed to try to recover the captives or secure peace, with a heart seed to be returned north if successful. Play ended peacefully in the nursery; no seed was received and no XP was awarded.

## What Happened

Before exploration, the group checked character sheets. Sab and Grond reported level four with 0/40 XP. Oogie reported level three with 14/30 XP, corrected a missing talent by adding two Wisdom to reach 16, and selected Fate as his tier-two spell. Orlin was allowed to begin rested after his player's absence.

Grond found the upper open-air passage blocked floor to ceiling by vines, leaves, flowers, and fungal growth. Oogie reached into its mycelial network and perceived an overgrown nursery inhabited by rats with moss, mushrooms, leaves, or thorns growing on them. A great pulsing plant dominated the room. He sensed no general aggression and told the others not to attack the rats. The group chose a dark upper hallway instead of cutting through the growth or returning downstairs.

Katherine Guy carried light near Grond. At a branching passage the party saw a large moss-furred rat with leaflike ears. Orlin focused the [[item-wooden-tongue-carving|Tongue of the Beast]] on it, but the rat fled from their light. As the group advanced, something brushed Oogie's feet in the darkness. Sab cast Light on her shield, revealing two blood-red, feathery centipedes about the length of a human arm behind them.

Grond wounded one centipede with his bow. It bit Gradrick for one damage; he resisted its poison. Sab killed the other with Smite. Orlin used a luck reroll, killed the wounded centipede with bear claws, and activated his [[item-claw-and-bone-talisman|Talisman of the Beastmaster]] to attract a nearby ordinary animal. The rat returned to the edge of the light, though the talisman's role in that return was not established.

Orlin spoke to the rat through the wooden tongue. It said that light hurt its eyes, asked whether the party were friends, and said Greenheart would decide. The group shielded or redirected its lights and followed the rat as it repeatedly showed itself farther along the corridor. They passed unfinished connectors and branching halls before reaching an old swollen door coated with rainbow-sheened mold.

Oogie and Katherine explored another branch and found an unexpectedly pristine door. Oogie opened it onto a warm, well-lit equipment room resembling the Locked and Loaded LLC storeroom from the party's first adventure. Lumpy, a rock gnome, sat reading behind a desk. He sold Oogie 60 feet of rope for one gold without explaining the doorway. Oogie stepped back into Quasqueton; the door immediately closed behind him and still looked new.

Grond forced the mold-covered door. Orlin called out peacefully and heard many rat voices, with one reassuring the others that the visitors had come to meet Greenheart. Inside, deep vegetation made walking difficult. A pod released a golden puff when Orlin stepped on it; no harmful effect was described. Bioluminescent fungi lit the walls, and the rats used two raised stone planters as nests. Some inhabitants looked thorny and potentially defensive, but none attacked.

Greenheart stood to the north beneath a large mushroom: an immense plant with three main chambers and smaller branching chambers, pulsing and glowing like a slow heartbeat. Grond entered with his weapons stowed. Sab covered her illuminated shield after the others entered, and Katherine snuffed her torch. Orlin continued reassuring the rats, while Gradrick stayed back and avoided stepping on them.

Sab drank the draught Mazzah had given Gradrick and understood Greenheart's heartbeat as a deep, motherly voice calming its children. She introduced herself as a devotee of Odin and said the party meant no harm. Greenheart said the many-eyed ones lived below, trapped its children with webs, and stole them. It wished to return to the north, from which the purple-robed one had taken it. Greenheart offered to provide a heart seed if the party returned the captives or secured peace, asking that the seed be returned to its home. Sab accepted conditionally, explaining that success and timing could not be promised.

At Greenheart's invitation, the group drank amber sap from a hanging flower bud. Their shared, fragmentary vision showed a wooded elven city built on the ground and in the trees. A purple-robed wizard with troops negotiated with a high elf, then stole a large three-chambered heart seed and fled as darkness descended over the forest. A later scene showed him experimenting on the seed in a laboratory. The vision ended with Greenheart's request to return a heart seed home. The party associated the wizard with Zelligar the Unknown; his identity was not independently established.

The group discussed how a seed might be transported and whether the rats would also leave. Greenheart would provide the seed after rescue or peace; the party could intuit that its children would prefer a more open, safe environment. No transport procedure, relocation, or departure occurred. Wade confirmed the party had generally agreed to try its best to return the captives or otherwise secure peace, and that the inhabitants feared fire and the webbed ones. Play stopped in the nursery, with the rats allowing friendly contact.
