# Northern Barrow

The Northern Barrow was the party's first substantial tomb expedition from Helix. Fresh tracks led the group here instead of to an older southern candidate mound.

Inside, the party fought a gray ooze, freed Ikram and Tiramel from a barred room, found evidence that Perry had died, and learned that Nabo had betrayed the Iron Chain expedition and escaped with Werner's sledgehammer. Those discoveries drove the pursuit that ended at the camp outside Helix.

The entry chamber, barred western room, and eastern chamber remain fully recorded in the vault, but their useful player-facing history is collected here. The site is explored and now belongs to the campaign archive rather than the active destination list.
