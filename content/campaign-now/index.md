# Campaign Now

## Previously On

The Hooded Lantern Cartel used Helix as its base while exploring the Barrow Mounds. Its pursuit of a stolen corpse led through Mouse's deliveries to Mort, Mara, and Tornar in the serpent-and-skull marked mound. Eldran Vey's coat and papers then drew the party across White Falls to Quasqueton.

## Where Things Stand

The party is in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] cleared upper tower with [[npc-katherine-guy|Katherine Guy]]. Its guardroom rest completed, and Grond's arrow followed by Oogie's cobra killed the remaining visible harpy in the nest. Three harpies have died across the approach and upper tower, with no further activity found nearby. The immediate harpy threat to outside provisioning is cleared; the wider stronghold's safety remains unproven.

Inside, a faint bitter incense smell and oversized rodent tracks lead toward unexplored areas. The [[item-writ-of-hollow-gate|Hollow Gate writ]] still supports a possible claim rather than established ownership. The entrance voice and unread Orcish blood message remain unresolved.

The marked-mound expedition has been reported to the Fair Church. Mort remains alive and last seen asleep in the mound; Bartholomew has said the church would welcome him if he returns. Mazzah holds Tornar's journals for study. The Wand of Unlife is destroyed.

## Threads in Motion

- **Quasqueton and the Hollow Gate writ:** Clearing the nest advances the party's interest in a usable stronghold, but the writ's legal force and the wider interior remain untested.
- **Activity deeper inside:** Dog-sized or larger rodent tracks cross the fountain hall and stairs. Faint incense comes from farther inside; its source and any connection to the tracks are unknown.
- **Unresolved entrance clues:** The voice's source and the Orcish blood message remain unknown. The possible waterfall hollow is unexamined.
- **Mort and the marked mound:** Mort remains asleep in the mound. The Fair Church knows what happened and remains willing to receive him; the north altar is still unexplored.
- **The Steel Bone Brotherhood:** A recurring pressure; the mound sessions did not prove Tornar's membership or Brotherhood ownership of his serpent-and-death practice.
- **Mazzah's research:** Tornar's journals may yield information about the necromancer or structures beneath the mounds.
- **Deferred destinations:** Castle Zentolin, Thornswild, the Broken-Sigil Mound, and the marked mound's altar remain available beyond this expedition.

## Recent Discoveries

- The fountain hall has a shallow emblem-bearing basin with an apparently plugged drain, an interior hallway, and stairs to the upper tower.
- Fresh seed husks and large rodent tracks show recent activity without revealing living occupants. The harpy nest also contained oversized rodent and humanoid bones.
- The cleared nest yielded 42 gold, Sab's silver-finished hand mirror, and Oogie's nonmagical sword-and-star buckle.
- The exterior offers edible berries and mushrooms, an old camp, and a possible waterfall hollow. Sab's fishing pole and hardened salt may support later provisioning.

## Current Regions and Destinations

- **Quasqueton** is the current expedition site; the party is in the cleared upper tower. Deeper rooms remain unexplored.
- **Helix** remains the expedition base and home of Mazzah, Vakish, the Fair Church, and the Brazen Strumpet.
- **Barrow Mounds** remain a recurring region. The marked mound's north altar and the Broken-Sigil Mound's remaining chambers are dormant return hooks.
- **Castle Zentolin** is a separate destination on the other road beyond White Falls, deferred in favor of Quasqueton.

## Party Intent

The group cleared the upper nest to make gathering food, wood, and torch material outside safer. Play resumes in the upper tower; no next route or return outside was established.
