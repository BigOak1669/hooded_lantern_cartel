# Campaign Now

## Previously On

The search for a stolen corpse in the Barrow Mounds led the Hooded Lantern Cartel to Mort, Mara, and Tornar. The damaged Hollow Gate writ and papers recovered with Eldran Vey, Quasqueton's former steward, drew the party out from Helix and across White Falls. At Quasqueton the group cleared the harpy nest, made peaceful contact with Greenheart, and encountered the silkbound in the upper passages.

## Where Things Stand

The party and [[npc-katherine-guy|Katherine Guy]] have returned to the first-floor entrance guardroom in [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton]]. They set up camp after finishing their upper-floor sweep. Play paused before a completed rest or a shared reading of the study papers and journals.

[[npc-greenheart|Greenheart]] still wants its children returned or peace secured with the web-using captors. Oogie, Sab, and Orlin saw a spiderlike controller through a silkbound hunter's psychic imagery. The hunter was killed in the upper corridor and a second silkbound was found dead by a corrosive pool. No captives or heart seed have been recovered.

The upper floor holds a smithy, torch-making materials, and a hidden repair cache. [[npc-lumpy|Lumpy's]] shop reopened in Session 23, and the party is unsure it will still be accessible on the next visit. The purple-curtained passage let Splat and items cross to [[npc-mazzah|Mazzah's]] sitting room and back. The tower-side attempt to reopen the drawn curtain failed. The [[item-writ-of-hollow-gate|Hollow Gate writ's]] legal force remains unresolved.

## Threads in Motion

- **[[thread-greenheart-captives|Greenheart's captives and heart seed]].** The controller's whereabouts and the captives' condition remain unknown. A broad stair leads to the first floor and a water shaft continues beyond it, but the party has yet to locate a route to the captors.
- **The hidden study.** Orlin and Gradrick brought back papers and journals. The portrait credits Marevak with guiding the builders. At camp the party plans to compare the loose note mentioning “IX” and the account of root experiments with what it learned about the silkbound controller and the stolen seed. Both connections are unresolved.
- **Quasqueton as a stronghold.** The upper resources make repairs and longer expeditions more plausible. The entrance voice, Orcish blood message, brass key's lock, and incense source remain open leads. The gray syrupy basin in the pool chamber has not been identified as the deadly pool from Oogie's earlier vision.
- **Mazzah and Helix.** Mazzah examined objects through the open curtain during the encounter. The party is unsure whether the doorway will still work. Mazzah does not know what caused it and plans to investigate. He also holds Tornar’s separate journals for study.
- **Mort and the Barrow Mounds.** Mort was last seen sleeping in the marked mound. The Fair Church knows and would welcome him back. The marked mound's north altar, the Broken-Sigil Mound, and the recurring Steel Bone Brotherhood pressure remain unresolved.

## Recent Discoveries

- The hidden study held the [[item-jewel-of-barbalt|Jewel of Barbalt]], two tactical healing potions, 90 gold, and a [[item-beetle-potion-of-animal-shifting|beetle transformation potion]]. Sab is carrying the jewel for safekeeping.
- Mazzah identified Sab's [[item-lion-faced-shield|Shield of the Lion]] as a +1 shield with a daily roar that intimidates nearby hostile creatures.
- Pale growth lit a familiar stretch of upper corridor. Usable charcoal and materials for torches remain in the service rooms. The hidden repair cache holds beams, doors, dressed stone, and rope.
- Lumpy's door reopened for the group and Sab bought 18 torches. The group also stocked up on oil and rations.

## Current Regions and Destinations

- **Quasqueton.** The entrance guardroom is the party's current campsite. The first-floor interior and deeper routes remain the next areas to explore. The possible hollow behind the exterior waterfall is still untested.
- **Helix.** The expedition base, home of the Fair Church, Mazzah, Vakish, and the Brazen Strumpet. The curtained threshold offers a possible shortcut.
- **The northern elven forest city.** Greenheart's reported home and the requested destination for the promised seed. The party has seen it in a vision. Its name, present state, and exact location remain unknown.
- **The Barrow Mounds, Castle Zentolin, and Thornswild.** These remain separate leads beyond the Quasqueton expedition.

## Party Intent

Study the recovered papers and journals overnight at camp, then explore the first-floor interior and pursue the silkbound lead. The group discussed later repairs and carousing once the stronghold is secure. Session 23 awarded seven XP.
