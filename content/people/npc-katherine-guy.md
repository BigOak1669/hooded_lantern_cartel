# Katherine Guy

Katherine Guy is the party's current torchbearer and camp helper. The group hired her at the [[location-brazen-strumpet|Brazen Strumpet]] for one gold per day, rising to two on a day involving combat.

She described herself as good with animals and camp work but poor with weapons. She carried expedition supplies, traveled beyond the Barrow Mounds, and reached [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] exterior with the party. She remained with the expedition through Session 18, though that session did not describe her actions or exact position during the approach, harpy fight, and entry.

In [[2026_0927 Session 19|Session 19]] she camped with the party, carried Sab's torch into the fountain hall, and expressed concern about confronting the remaining harpies. She waited quietly near the upper stairs as instructed, then joined the group after the fight. She is alive and with the party in the cleared upper tower.
