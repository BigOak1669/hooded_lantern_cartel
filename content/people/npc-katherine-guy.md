# Katherine Guy

Katherine Guy is the party's current torchbearer and camp helper. The group hired her at the [[location-brazen-strumpet|Brazen Strumpet]] for one gold per day, rising to two on a day involving combat.

She described herself as good with animals and camp work but poor with weapons. She carried expedition supplies, traveled beyond the Barrow Mounds, and reached [[location-quasqueton|Quasqueton's]] exterior with the party. She remains with the expedition after Session 18. Her actions and exact position during the approach, harpy fight, and entry were not described.
